# The Last Leaf (15 s)

Logline: Clawd wants to catch the last leaf of autumn, but the wind keeps snatching it away, so Clawd gives up, and that's when it drifts down and lands on Clawd's head like a hat.

World: an autumn meadow with one bare tree, golden-hour light. Palette: pale gold sky, olive grass, brown bark, one crimson leaf.
Colour arc: the sky slides from pale gold to dusky rose across the film.

Motif: the leaf. It opens the film, alone on its branch, and closes it, sitting on Clawd's head (the same close framing).

Clawd's arc: hopeful → excited → determined → sad → surprised → happy.
